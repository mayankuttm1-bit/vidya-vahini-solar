TopCon vs Mono PERC Solar Panels: Which Generates Higher Yield in UP Summer Heat?

Why temperature coefficient matters when ambient temperatures in Prayagraj exceed 45°C. Comparing N-Type tunnel oxide passivated contact cells with legacy P-Type modules for 30-year energy yield.

1. The Heat Problem in Prayagraj Summers

Solar panels are standardly rated at 25°C cell temperature (Standard Test Conditions - STC). However, during May and June in Prayagraj and Eastern Uttar Pradesh, rooftop module temperatures frequently soar to 65°C to 72°C.

As a semiconductor heats up, its electrical resistance increases, leading to a drop in voltage and total power output. This rate of power loss is governed by the panel's Temperature Coefficient of Pmax (%/°C).
