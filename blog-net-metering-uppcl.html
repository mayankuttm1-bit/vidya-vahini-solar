Step-by-Step Guide: Net-Metering Approval Process with UPPCL in Prayagraj

Everything consumers need to know about setting up a bi-directional solar net-meter with Purvanchal Vidyut Vitran Nigam Limited (PuVVNL). From technical feasibility sanction to meter testing and Joint Commissioning Reports (JCR).

1. What is Net-Metering in Uttar Pradesh?

Net-metering allows your rooftop solar plant to connect directly to the electricity grid via a specialized bi-directional energy meter. During bright daylight hours, when your solar panels generate more electricity than your home consumes, the excess power automatically flows into the UPPCL grid, running your meter in reverse.

At the end of your billing cycle, UPPCL deducts the units you exported from the units you consumed. If you generated more than you consumed, the surplus credit carries forward into your next month’s bill!

2. The 5 Stages of UPPCL Approval in Prayagraj

Stage 1: Sanctioned Load Verification

Under UPERC regulations, your solar plant capacity cannot exceed 100% of your sanctioned load. For instance, to install a 3 kW solar plant, your electricity connection must be sanctioned for at least 3 kW. Vidya Vahini handles load enhancement applications if your connection is currently 1 kW or 2 kW.

Stage 2: Technical Feasibility Sanction (TFS)

Submitted through the National Solar Portal. The local subdivision Executive Engineer (EE) verifies transformer capacity and sanctions feasibility within 7 working days.

Stage 3: Procurement & Lab Testing of Bi-Directional Meter

A certified DLMS-compliant bi-directional meter is procured and sent to the official UPPCL meter testing laboratory in Prayagraj for precision accuracy testing and official lead sealing.

Stage 4: Physical Meter Replacement

The local DISCOM Junior Engineer (JE) removes the old uni-directional meter and commissions the new bi-directional net meter.

Stage 5: Joint Commissioning Report (JCR)

A joint report is signed between consumer, vendor, and UPPCL engineer. Once uploaded to the portal, DBT subsidy disbursal triggers automatically.
