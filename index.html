CLARITY & ASSURANCE

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about rooftop solar, flour mills, subsidies, and net metering.

Net metering replaces your regular electricity meter with a bi-directional digital meter. During the day, your solar panels generate power; whatever you don't consume is automatically exported into the UPPCL grid. At night, you draw power from the grid. At the end of the billing cycle, you only pay for the net units consumed (Import minus Export). If you export more than you use, the credits carry forward.
A standard 10 HP diesel flour mill consumes around 1.5 to 2.0 liters of diesel per operating hour. Running 7 to 8 hours daily costs roughly ₹1,200 to ₹1,500 every single day (₹35,000 to ₹45,000 per month). By switching to an 18kW-20kW solar system, your fuel cost drops to virtually ₹0. Most mill operators recover their entire capital investment within 2.0 to 2.5 years.
The subsidy is paid as a Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) directly from the Ministry of New and Renewable Energy (MNRE) and the UP State Government straight into your Aadhaar-linked bank account. Once we complete your installation and UPPCL commissions the net meter, our team uploads the commissioning report on the National Portal. The subsidy is typically disbursed within 25 to 30 working days.
Our systems use N-Type TopCon and Mono PERC bifacial solar panels from Loom Solar, which feature advanced low-light absorption technology. Even on overcast days, the panels generate 25% to 40% of their peak capacity. Because on-grid systems remain connected to the electricity grid, the power shortfall is automatically compensated by grid power without any flicker.
Solar PV panels come with a 25-Year Manufacturer Linear Performance Warranty (guaranteeing over 80% power output at Year 25) and a 10 to 12 Year Product Warranty. Inverters come with a standard 5 to 7 year warranty (extendable up to 10 years). In addition, Vidya Vahini Solar Agency provides a comprehensive 5-year workmanship and on-site maintenance warranty.
