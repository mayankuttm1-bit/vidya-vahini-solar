OUR HERITAGE & MISSION

A Decade of Solar Leadership & Engineering Integrity in Prayagraj

Starting as a grassroots clean energy initiative in rural Koraon in 2014, Vidya Vahini Solar Agency has evolved into a premier EPC contractor and formalized corporate entity, Risaenergy Private Limited.

OUR 10-YEAR JOURNEY

From Rural Solarization to Corporate EPC

2014

Foundation in Koraon

Founded in Koraon, Prayagraj by the promoters, providing off-grid solar kits, battery backups, and rural lighting solutions to villages suffering from frequent power blackouts.

2018

Pioneering Solar Atta Chakkis

Recognizing the crippling fuel cost of diesel flour mills in rural agricultural areas, our engineers designed high-torque VFD solar flour mill drives, transforming local village micro-economies.

2021

Formal GST Registration & Naini Headquarters

Established official commercial headquarters at 10/C 4A Madhavpur Patti, Kharakauni, Naini, Prayagraj and registered under GST (`09GLQPS9587D2ZX`) to service urban rooftop solar projects.

2024

National PM Surya Ghar Empanelment

Empaneled under the national PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana rooftop solar scheme via UPNEDA portal, facilitating direct Central and UP State DBT subsidies for hundreds of residential homeowners.

Sept 2026

Incorporation of Risaenergy Private Limited

To scale institutional EPC operations and utility-scale tenders, the promoters officially incorporated Risaenergy Private Limited (CIN: `U35105UP2026PTC252185`) with the Registrar of Companies (ROC Uttar Pradesh).
