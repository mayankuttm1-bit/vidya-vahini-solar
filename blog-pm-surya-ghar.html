  • Maximum Direct Subsidy: ₹1,08,000 for residential systems of 3 kW capacity and above (₹78,000 Central + ₹30,000 UP State Top-Up).
  • Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT): The subsidy amount is deposited directly into your bank account within 30 days of net-meter commissioning.

1. What is the PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana?

Launched with a national outlay of ₹75,021 Crores, the PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana aims to power 1 Crore Indian households with rooftop solar by providing up to 300 free units of electricity every month.

In Uttar Pradesh, the initiative is implemented through the Uttar Pradesh New and Renewable Energy Development Agency (UPNEDA) and local distribution companies under UPPCL (such as Purvanchal Vidyut Vitran Nigam Limited - PuVVNL). What makes Uttar Pradesh particularly attractive for rooftop solar is the state's additional top-up incentive of ₹15,000 to ₹30,000 over and above the Central Government’s subsidy.

4. Step-by-Step Guide: How to Apply & Receive Subsidy in Prayagraj

At Vidya Vahini Solar Agency, our engineering liaison team manages the entire process end-to-end on behalf of our clients. Here is the step-by-step roadmap:

1

National Portal Registration

Register on pmsuryaghar.gov.in with your State (Uttar Pradesh), Electricity Distribution Company (PuVVNL / UPPCL), and Consumer Account Number from your latest electricity bill.

2

DISCOM Technical Feasibility Approval

UPPCL verifies whether your local distribution transformer has sufficient hosting capacity and approves the technical feasibility within 3 to 7 working days.

3

Vendor Selection & Physical Installation

Select Vidya Vahini Solar Agency / Risaenergy Pvt Ltd as your registered empaneled vendor on the portal. Our technical team delivers Tier-1 ALMM-listed TopCon modules, on-grid inverters, and hot-dip galvanized mounting structures engineered to withstand 150 km/h wind speeds.

4

Net-Meter Testing, Sealing & Installation

A bi-directional net-meter is procured and tested at the official UPPCL testing lab in Prayagraj. The DISCOM Junior Engineer (JE) visits your premises to inspect the installation, seal the meter, and submit the Joint Inspection Report (JCR).

5

Direct Subsidy Credit to Your Bank Account

Once the commissioning certificate is issued, you upload a copy of your cancelled cheque. The Central Government subsidy (₹78,000) and UP State subsidy (₹30,000) are credited directly via DBT within 30 days.

5. Mandatory Documents Checklist

Ensure you have the following documents ready before initiating your application:

Latest UPPCL Electricity Bill

Must be in the name of the property owner with zero dues.

Aadhaar Card of Consumer

Name must match the electricity bill account record.

Cancelled Bank Cheque

Account holder name, IFSC code, and account number must be clearly printed.

Clear Rooftop Photograph

Photograph of your terrace showing shadow-free orientation.
